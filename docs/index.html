CEC Remote Remap

Help for NVIDIA Shield and other Android TV devices.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and follow the two quick setup steps. The app takes you straight to the right place in Settings each time.
  2. Choose Add a button and press the button on your TV remote that you want to change.
  3. Pick what Press, Double press and Hold should do for that button.
  4. Not fast enough or too fast? Change Hold time and Double-press speed under Options.

Free and Pro

Free: one remote button with a Press action, so you can check it works with your TV first.

Pro: a one-time purchase through Google Play that unlocks unlimited buttons, Double press, Hold and the timing options. No subscription.

Payments and refunds are handled by Google Play. If Pro is refunded, the app goes back to the free version.

Troubleshooting

  • Nothing happens when I press a button in Add a button. Your TV keeps that button for itself and doesn't send it to the Shield. Most TVs do this with Home, Settings, Source and Volume. Try another button.
  • The buttons stopped working. Open the app once and check that both setup steps still show as turned on.
  • A Hold also runs the Press action first. Some TVs send a held button that way. It comes from the TV, not the app.
  • Typing doesn't work. Your normal on-screen keyboard is used for typing as always. If it doesn't appear, open the app once.

Privacy

Nothing you see or type ever leaves your device. Your settings are stored only on your TV. The only internet use is Google Play's purchase system for the Pro unlock. The full privacy policy is linked on the app's Google Play page.
