# 📜 Privacy Policy for CEC Remote Remap

**Effective Date:** November 9, 2026

CEC Remote Remap is a utility for **NVIDIA Shield** and other **Android TV** devices that lets users remap the buttons on their TV remote (HDMI-CEC). Everything the app does with your buttons, your screen and your typing happens on your device and never leaves it. The only part that uses the internet is **Google Play's purchase system**, which handles the optional Pro unlock (see section 5).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

CEC Remote Remap does **not** collect any personal information.

### A. Locally Stored Data (On Your Device)
- **Button Settings** – The remote buttons you add and the actions you assign to them (Press, Double press and Hold). This data is created by you and stored strictly on your device.
- **Options** – Your selected Hold time and Double-press speed.
- **Pro Purchase Receipt** – If you buy Pro, the app keeps Google Play's signed receipt on your device so Pro keeps working offline. It contains an order number and a purchase token, not your name, email or payment details.
- **Keyboard Preference** – The name of your normal on-screen keyboard, so typing keeps working as usual.

We do **not** collect:
- Your name, email address, phone number, or location.
- Anything you type, watch or see on your screen.
- Any usage statistics, analytics or crash reports of our own.

---

## 2. Accessibility Service

CEC Remote Remap uses Android's **Accessibility Service** only to:
- Perform the actions you assign to remote buttons (Home, Back, Recent apps, Sleep and Screenshot).
- Keep your normal on-screen keyboard working while the app is on.
- Take you to the right place in Settings during setup.

The app does **not** read, record or store what is on your screen.

---

## 3. Keyboard (Input Method)

The app includes a keyboard component that it needs to receive your remote's buttons. It never shows a keyboard and never sees what you type. When you type, your normal keyboard is used as always.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

All data is stored **locally on your device** inside the app.
- There are no user accounts and nothing is synced to any server of ours.
- Data is kept only as long as the app is installed.

You may delete **all stored data** at any time with **Reset everything** in the app, by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

---

## 5. Google Play Purchases (Pro)

The app is free to use with one button. **Pro** is an optional one-time purchase made through **Google Play**.
- Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer never sees your card or payment details.
- The app asks Google Play on your device whether Pro is owned on your Google account, so Pro can be switched back off if a purchase is refunded.
- To work, Google's Play Billing Library (included in the app) sends Google basic technical information about how purchase requests went (for example whether a request succeeded or failed). This is handled by Google under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). It never includes your button settings, anything on your screen or anything you type.

---

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

CEC Remote Remap does **not** share, sell, or send any of your data to anyone, including the developer. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only outside service is Google Play's purchase system described above.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

CEC Remote Remap is a general utility and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
