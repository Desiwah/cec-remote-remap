# 📜 Privacy Policy for CEC Remote Remap

**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026

CEC Remote Remap is a utility for **NVIDIA Shield** and other **Android TV** devices that lets users remap the buttons on their TV remote (HDMI-CEC). The app works completely offline. It does **not** have internet access and cannot send any data off your device.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

CEC Remote Remap does **not** collect any personal information.

### A. Locally Stored Data (On Your Device)
- **Button Settings** – The remote buttons you add and the actions you assign to them (Press, Double press and Hold). This data is created by you and stored strictly on your device.
- **Options** – Your selected Hold time and Double-press speed.
- **Keyboard Preference** – The name of your normal on-screen keyboard, so the app can hand over to it when you start typing.

We do **not** collect:
- Your name, email address, phone number, or location.
- Anything you type, watch or see on your screen.
- Any usage statistics, analytics or crash reports.

---

## 2. Accessibility Service

CEC Remote Remap uses Android's **Accessibility Service** only to:
- Perform the actions you assign to remote buttons (Home, Back, Recent apps, Sleep and Screenshot).
- Notice when your on-screen keyboard closes, so the app can go back to catching remote buttons.
- Take you to the right place in Settings during setup.

The app does **not** read, record or store what is on your screen.

---

## 3. Keyboard (Input Method)

Android TV sends remote buttons through the active keyboard, so the app includes an invisible keyboard that catches them. It never shows a keyboard and never sees what you type. As soon as you start typing, it hands over to your normal keyboard.

---

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

All data is stored **locally on your device** inside the app.
- There are no user accounts and nothing is synced to any server.
- Data is kept only as long as the app is installed.

You may delete **all stored data** at any time with **Reset everything** in the app, by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

---

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

CEC Remote Remap does **not** share, sell, or transmit any data to anyone, including the developer. The app has no third-party services, ads or analytics.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

CEC Remote Remap is a general utility and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
